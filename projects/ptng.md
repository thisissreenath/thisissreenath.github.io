## Overview

For my undergraduate dissertation at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, our team investigated a shoe-sole-integrated **piezoelectric–triboelectric nanogenerator (PTNG)** intended to harvest energy from walking.

The work explored materials, layer arrangements, and device configurations for converting mechanical motion into electrical energy. The project was guided by Dr. Sreenidhi P. R. and Baby Sreeja S. D.

## My contributions

- Led a five-member student team through design research, modeling, and prototyping.
- Compared candidate piezoelectric and triboelectric material configurations and investigated structural design considerations.
- Used **COMSOL Multiphysics** to study electric-field and potential distributions under different structural conditions.
- Contributed to prototype development, including energy-harvesting circuitry and energy-storage considerations.
- Presented the project at **ICMSMT 2021**.

## Methods and tools

Energy harvesting · Piezoelectricity · Triboelectricity · COMSOL Multiphysics · Prototype development · Team leadership

## What this work demonstrates

Research-led engineering from materials evaluation and multiphysics modeling through prototype implementation and technical presentation.
