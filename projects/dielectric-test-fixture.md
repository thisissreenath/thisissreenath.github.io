## Overview

Repeatable dielectric insulation testing requires specimen positioning, stable setup geometry, and equipment integration. At Virginia Tech CPES, I designed and assembled practical test fixtures to support high-voltage insulation experiments.

## My contributions

- Designed and assembled **lower-cost dielectric testing fixtures**, bringing the estimated unit cost from **approximately $100 to $17 (~83% reduction)**.
- Considered specimen positioning, preparation time, and repeatability in the fixture design.
- Supported integration with the laboratory high-voltage testing workflow and documented practical setup procedures.

## Methods and tools

Fixture design · Laboratory prototyping · Dielectric testing · Cost-conscious engineering · Experimental repeatability · Technical documentation

## What this work demonstrates

Pragmatic engineering design that lowers experimental hardware cost while supporting repeatable laboratory workflows.
