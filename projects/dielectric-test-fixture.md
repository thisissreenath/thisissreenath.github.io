## Overview

Repeatable electrical testing depends not only on measurement equipment but also on practical specimen positioning and fixture setup. At the Center for Power Electronics Systems (CPES), Virginia Tech, I developed a laboratory fixture to support dielectric insulation experiments.

## My contributions

- Considered specimen positioning, ease of setup, and repeatability when developing the test fixture.
- Designed and fabricated a **3D-printed fixture** as a practical laboratory alternative.
- Used the fixture to support consistent test preparation and equipment integration.
- Documented its setup and use to make experimental workflows easier to reproduce.

## Methods and tools

Fixture design · Additive manufacturing · Laboratory testing · Test setup repeatability · Technical documentation

## What this work demonstrates

Applying design-for-use principles to experimental hardware and creating practical tools for laboratory research.
