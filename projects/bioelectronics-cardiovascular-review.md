## Overview

This literature review explores the role of bioelectronic technologies in cardiovascular disease monitoring, diagnosis, and treatment. It examines how electronic devices can interface with biological systems, with attention to wearable sensors, implantable devices, and therapeutic applications.

## Topics covered

- **Wearable and implantable sensing:** physiological monitoring and interfaces with biological tissue.
- **Cardiac devices:** electroanatomic mapping, pacemakers, implantable defibrillators, cardiac resynchronization therapy, and ventricular assist devices.
- **Emerging approaches:** soft bioelectronics, closed-loop systems, bioresorbable electronics, and bioprinting.
- **Engineering challenges:** biocompatibility, miniaturization, power management, long-term stability, regulatory considerations, and ethical issues.

## Research approach

I synthesized and compared published literature to identify common design considerations, technological developments, and open challenges in cardiovascular bioelectronics.

## Nature of the work

This is a **literature-based review**, not an experimental device-development project or a peer-reviewed publication.
