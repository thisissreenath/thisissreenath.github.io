## Overview

This Virginia Tech academic project examined the embedded package design of a **100 V gallium nitride high-electron-mobility transistor (GaN HEMT)** for electric-vehicle power-converter applications. I led a **four-person design team** under the guidance of Dr. Christina DiMarino.

## My contributions

- Led coordination of the four-person embedded-package design study.
- Contributed **insulator selection** and **PCB thermal analysis** to address electrical and thermal constraints.
- The **team's simulations** achieved **0.488 nH power-loop inductance** compared with a **0.625 nH design target**.
- Conducted physical **wedge-bond pull** and **solder die-attach shear** experiments under different process settings; assessed observed bond failures using **MIL-STD-883** methods.

## Engineering methods

Package design · Material selection · PCB thermal analysis · Parasitic inductance · Bond pull testing · Die-attach shear testing · MIL-STD-883

## What this work demonstrates

Technical leadership and cross-disciplinary collaboration in power-module packaging, together with hands-on mechanical qualification of assembly processes.
