During my undergraduate studies at Amrita School of Engineering, I led a team of six students in organizing a visit through our **Student Social Responsibility** initiative. Our goal was to spend time with children at a local residential care organization and contribute useful educational and recreational resources.

## Organizing the initiative

I helped coordinate the team, plan activities, and organize learning materials. During the visit, we spoke with the children about their interests and aspirations, shared educational resources such as books and study guides, and provided sports equipment to support recreational activities.

The day also included informal conversations and a shared meal. This experience strengthened my understanding of teamwork, community involvement, and the importance of listening before deciding how to contribute.
